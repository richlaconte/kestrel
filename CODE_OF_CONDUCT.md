# Code of conduct

Kestrel follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be welcoming and kind, assume good faith, criticize ideas rather than people,
and accept that maintainers will remove comments, commits or contributors that don't.

It applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere you
represent the project.

## Reporting

To report behavior that breaks it, contact the maintainer privately through the email on
their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/richlaconte). Reports are kept confidential.
Maintainers decide on consequences, from a warning to a ban, as the Covenant's
enforcement guidelines describe.
